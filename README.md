## Source Devices Dismantled
1. **Audio Receiver / Amplifier Unit**
   * *Extracted:* Heavy extruded aluminum heatsink, high-capacity capacitors, multi-conductor wire harness, multi-position switches.
2. **Xbox 360 Game Controllers**
   * *Extracted:* Rumble vibration motors, self-tapping casing screws.
3. **iPhone 5 and 7 and Android Tablet Chassis**
   * *Extracted:* Micro-screws, internal mounting brackets (logic board & Li-ion battery logged for e-waste disposal).
4. **Mechanical Keyboard & Miscellaneous Gear**
   * *Extracted:* High-flex USB cables, compression springs.

## E-Waste Management
* **Lithium-Ion Batteries:** Terminals taped and isolated in Ziploc bags for drop-off at a certified battery recycling facility.
* **Damaged High-Voltage Components:** The main power transformer was very damaged. Better to discard than to risk reusing it.
* **General E-Waste:** Bare PCBs, cracked chassis plastics, and heavy shielded cable tangles set aside for drop-off at local retail e-waste collection bins (Best Buy / Home Depot / NYC Sanitation Department BK11).

---

## Key Technical Insights Learned
1. **Wire Types matter:** Stranded wire from consumer cables is significantly better for moving joints and robotics than solid-core wire because it resists metal fatigue and snapping.
2. **Manufacturing Fasteners:** Standard Phillips/Flatheads give way to Security Torx in consumer gaming consoles to prevent unauthorized tampering.
3. **Chemicals like isopropyl alochol** helps remove old glue.
